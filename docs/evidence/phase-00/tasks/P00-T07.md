## Security and privacy

Only secret names appear; no secret values, credentials, contacts, or production data are stored.

## Rollout and rollback

Governance-only. Review dates and mitigations are updated through the normal change process; closing a risk requires evidence.

## Risks and follow-up

The sole developer/repository owner validates severity, triggers, mitigations, review dates, vendor assessments, and exit plans.
